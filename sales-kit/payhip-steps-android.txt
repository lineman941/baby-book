SELLING WITHOUT ETSY - PAYHIP (Android)
========================================

Payhip costs nothing to start, has no listing fee, and does
not gatekeep new sellers the way Etsy does. You can be
taking payments today.


PART 1 - MAKE THE ACCOUNT
--------------------------
1. Chrome -> payhip.com -> Sign Up (free).
2. Confirm your email.
3. Skip any "upgrade" offers. The free plan is fine.
   (Free plan takes 5% per sale. No monthly cost.)


PART 2 - CONNECT HOW YOU GET PAID
----------------------------------
Settings -> Payment methods.
Connect PayPal OR Stripe. PayPal is faster if you already
have one. You cannot get paid until this is done.


PART 3 - CREATE THE PRODUCT
----------------------------
1. Products -> Add New Product -> DIGITAL PRODUCT
2. Upload BOTH files from "1 SELL THIS (upload to store)":
      OurLittleMiracle.html
      welcome-guide.pdf
3. Open PAYHIP-listing-text.md (folder "3 WHAT TO PASTE")
   and copy in:
      - Product name
      - Description
4. Price: 14.97      Compare-at price: 39.97
5. Cover image: photo-1-MAIN.jpg from "2 LISTING PHOTOS"
   Add the other 4 photos and listing-video.mp4 too.
6. Publish.

You now have a link like payhip.com/b/XXXX
That link IS your store. Send me the link.


PART 4 - GETTING BUYERS (this is the real work)
-----------------------------------------------
Payhip gives you a link but no shoppers. Etsy would have
brought some. So traffic has to come from posting:

  - Pinterest: 22 ready-made pins in "4 MARKETING PINS".
    Pinterest is where baby-book buyers actually search.
    Post 2-3 a day, link each to your Payhip link.
  - TikTok: 30 scripts in the marketing kit. Use the
    listing video as your first post.
  - Facebook groups for new/expecting moms. Read the
    rules first - many ban selling outright.


ABOUT THE ETSY SUSPENSION
--------------------------
Do not open a second Etsy account to get around it. Etsy
detects that by device and payment details, and it turns a
temporary suspension into a permanent ban.

Appeal the one you have. Tell me exactly what the email or
banner said and I will help you write the appeal.
