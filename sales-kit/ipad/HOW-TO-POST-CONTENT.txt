HOW TO APPROVE AND POST A TIKTOK VIDEO
=========================================

WHERE: Right here. This same chat with Claude. There is no
separate app, dashboard, or website for this - it all happens
in this conversation.


STEP 1 - ASK FOR A CLIP
-------------------------
Just type one of these:

  "Post the next TikTok clip"
  "Generate script #5"          (pick any number from
                                  marketing-kit/tiktok-scripts.md)
  "What scenes are ready to post?"

Claude builds it (takes under a minute) and SENDS YOU THE ACTUAL
VIDEO FILE right here in the chat. Not a description of it - the
real file, so you can watch it before anything goes anywhere.


STEP 2 - WATCH IT
-------------------
Open the video. If anything looks wrong - a typo, a photo that
didn't load, the wrong music vibe - just say so. Nothing posts
until you say it's good. There's no undo on a public TikTok post,
so this is the moment that matters.


STEP 3 - ANSWER THREE QUICK QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------
Claude will ask, right in the chat:

  1. Who sees it first?
     - "Public" (normal, once you trust the clip)
     - "Only me" (posts privately so you can check it on your
        own profile before making it public - good for the
        first few)

  2. Confirm it's labeled as promoting your own product.
     This isn't optional - TikTok requires it by law for any ad,
     and it's your own product, so it's always "yes." Claude
     asks because TikTok's rules say a human has to confirm it,
     not because there's a real choice here.

  3. Want trending music on it?
     Usually yes - it helps the video get seen. Claude will show
     you 3-4 real trending options to pick from, with a link to
     listen first.

Just answer in plain words - "public, yes, yes" is a complete
answer.


STEP 4 - IT POSTS
--------------------
Claude posts it for real, right then, and gives you the link.
That's the whole process.


WANT TO DO MORE THAN ONE?
----------------------------
Don't post more than 1 a day - TikTok likes a steady pace, not a
burst. There are 30 scripts already written in
marketing-kit/tiktok-scripts.md - that's a month of content ready
to go, one message at a time.


IF YOU SEE A GENERATED CLIP YOU'RE NOT READY TO POST YET
-------------------------------------------------------------
Say "hold that one" - Claude keeps it waiting (it's tracked in
the project so nothing gets lost) and you can come back to it
whenever.
