LISTING ON ETSY FROM YOUR ANDROID PHONE
========================================

Do these in order. Don't skip PART 1.


PART 1 - GET THE FILES OUT OF THE ZIP
--------------------------------------
1. Tap BABYBOOK.zip in this chat. It downloads.
2. Open your "Files" app (some phones call it "My Files").
3. Tap "Downloads".
4. Tap BABYBOOK.zip. Choose EXTRACT (or "Extract to").
5. You now have a BABYBOOK folder with 4 numbered folders inside.


PART 2 - TEST IT (30 seconds, do not skip)
-------------------------------------------
1. Open folder "1 SELL THIS (upload to store)"
2. Tap OurLittleMiracle.html
3. If it asks which app, choose CHROME.
4. Type a name on the cover, tap Save Cover.
5. Tap "Go to My Book" - you should land on the dashboard.

That is exactly what your buyer gets. If anything looks
wrong, stop and tell me before listing.


PART 3 - OPEN ETSY THE RIGHT WAY
---------------------------------
Use CHROME, not the Etsy app. The Etsy app cannot set up
digital downloads properly.

1. Open Chrome. Go to etsy.com. Sign in.
2. Tap the three dots (top right).
3. Tick "Desktop site".
   The page will look small - pinch to zoom. That is normal
   and it is the only way the listing form works right.


PART 4 - START THE LISTING
---------------------------
1. Tap the shop icon / menu -> "Shop Manager"
2. Tap "Listings"
3. Tap "Add a listing"

If Etsy asks you to open a shop first, it will walk you
through shop name, country and currency. Pick anything
sensible; you can change the shop name later.


PART 5 - FILL IT IN
--------------------
Open ETSY-PASTE-THIS.txt (in folder "3 WHAT TO PASTE")
in a second Chrome tab so you can copy from it.

  PHOTOS    Upload all 5 from "2 LISTING PHOTOS".
            photo-1-MAIN.jpg goes FIRST.

  TITLE     Copy STEP 1 from the paste file.

  ABOUT     Who made it:  I did
            What is it:   A finished product
            When:         Made to order

  CATEGORY  Search "baby book" and pick a digital /
            template category.

  TYPE      *** DIGITAL ***  (this is the important one)

  PRICE     14.97
  QUANTITY  999

  DESCRIPTION  Copy STEP 4 from the paste file.
               It is long. Copy the whole thing.

  TAGS      13 of them, in STEP 5. Add one at a time.

  FILES     Upload BOTH from "1 SELL THIS":
              OurLittleMiracle.html
              welcome-guide.pdf


PART 6 - PUBLISH
-----------------
Tap "Publish". Etsy charges $0.20 per listing.

You are live.


AFTER YOU PUBLISH
------------------
Send me the listing link. Next steps are the Pinterest
pins in folder "4 MARKETING PINS" - that is where your
first traffic comes from.


IF YOU GET STUCK
-----------------
Tell me the exact words on the screen and I will tell you
what to tap. Do not guess and do not start over.
