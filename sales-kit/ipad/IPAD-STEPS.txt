HOW TO DO THIS ON YOUR iPAD
============================

PART 1 - GET THE FILES OUT OF THE ZIP
--------------------------------------
1. Tap the BABYBOOK.zip I sent. It saves to Files.
2. Open the FILES app (blue folder icon).
3. Tap "Downloads" (or "On My iPad").
4. TAP THE ZIP ONCE. It unzips into a folder called BABYBOOK.
5. Tap that folder. You'll see the 4 numbered folders.

PART 2 - TEST YOUR PRODUCT (30 seconds)
----------------------------------------
1. In folder "1 SELL THIS (upload to store)", tap OurLittleMiracle.html
2. It opens and shows the baby book.
   (If it only previews, tap the Share icon > "Open in Safari".)
3. Type a name on the cover. That's your product working.

PART 3 - CREATE THE ETSY LISTING
---------------------------------
1. Open SAFARI (not the Etsy app - the app can't do
   digital downloads properly).
2. Go to etsy.com and sign in.
3. IMPORTANT: tap the "aA" in the address bar and choose
   "Request Desktop Website". Etsy's listing form needs this.
4. Go to: Shop Manager > Listings > Add a listing
5. Open ETSY-PASTE-THIS.txt and follow steps 1-7 in it.

UPLOADING FILES ON iPAD
------------------------
When Etsy asks for photos or files, tap "Choose File" then
"Browse", then go to Downloads > BABYBOOK > the right folder.
